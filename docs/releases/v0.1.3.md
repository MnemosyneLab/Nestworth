# v0.1.3 Release Contract

## Purpose and Status

**Release theme:** Activity and History — understand how wealth changes.

**Status:** `Planned`. Product scope and technical decisions are approved by this document set; implementation has not started.

v0.1.3 adds an auditable local Activity ledger and a trustworthy history boundary on top of the v0.1.2 balance sheet and valuation system. It explains recorded changes without pretending that pre-v0.1.3 current-state edits were trades.

The detailed implementation contract is split between this release scope, the [technical design](v0.1.3-technical-design.md), and the [implementation plan](v0.1.3-implementation-plan.md).

## Release Goal

A Household can record why account balances, cash, debt, and investment quantities changed; inspect those changes by Account and date; and view a daily net-worth trend whose incomplete points remain explicit.

The central invariant is:

> A Household-internal transfer changes the participating Accounts but contributes zero external wealth flow.

## User Stories

- As a user, I can record external deposits and withdrawals without confusing them with internal movement.
- As a user, I can transfer cash or an Instrument position between Household Accounts as one atomic Activity.
- As an investor, I can record a Buy or Sell with quantity, execution price, settlement cash, and fees.
- As a user, I can record income, fees, debt draws, debt payments, balance reconciliation, position adjustment, and manual valuation.
- As a user, I can reverse an incorrect Activity or replace it with a correction without rewriting audit history.
- As a user, I can filter Activities and inspect the timeline for one Account.
- As a user, I can see daily net-worth history with exact quote provenance and visible gaps when historical inputs are missing.
- As an existing v0.1.2 user, I can see where trustworthy history starts without seeing fabricated deposits or trades.
- As an offline user, I can maintain all v0.1.3 facts manually without a market-data provider.

## Included

### Activity Ledger

- Immutable Activities composed of one header and one or more validated legs.
- Every persisted leg has a positive magnitude; creating an empty component or submitting an unchanged adjustment creates no Activity.
- Adjustment, Deposit, Withdrawal, Transfer, Buy, Sell, Income, Fee, Debt Draw, Debt Payment, Debt Adjustment, and Manual Valuation workflows.
- Cash transfers between Balance Accounts and Holdings-account cash components.
- Position transfers between Holdings Accounts for the same Instrument and exact Quantity.
- Same-currency and explicit cross-currency cash transfers.
- Atomic projection of accepted Activities into current Account Value, Account Cash, and Holding Quantity state.
- Stable classification of external flow, internal movement, income, fee, trade principal, debt principal, remeasurement, market-price movement, and FX movement.

### Corrections

- No edit or delete command for a posted Activity.
- Reversal as a new Activity containing the exact inverse effects.
- Correction as one transaction that posts a reversal and a replacement Activity.
- Original, reversal, and replacement records remain visible and linked.
- Backdated posting or correction invalidates affected daily snapshots from its effective date forward.

### History Origin and Compatibility

- One History Origin per Household. Its captured financial baseline is immutable; an unconfirmed UTC timezone fallback may be confirmed once before the first Activity or persisted snapshot.
- A migrated v0.1.2 Household captures current Accounts, values, Holdings, cash, relevant Account state, Ownership, and quote preferences as an origin baseline.
- Baseline items are not Activities and are labeled as opening state.
- Existing Account Value, Account Cash, Instrument Quote, and FX Quote observations retain their original identities and timestamps.
- Existing Holding Quantity is captured as current state only; it is never interpreted as a Buy, Sell, Transfer, or Adjustment.
- Trustworthy reconstructed daily history starts at the History Origin. Older observations may remain visible as legacy observations but do not create a pre-origin net-worth trend.

### Historical Valuation

- Historical Instrument Quote and FX Quote selection at or before each daily cutoff.
- Current-day live valuation plus persisted snapshots for closed local calendar days.
- Daily Household totals and component rows with native value, base value, selected quote references, completeness, and missing-input reasons.
- Append-only snapshot revisions when a backdated Activity, quote, preference change, or correction changes a previously calculated day.
- Partial numeric totals when some components are unavailable; missing components are excluded and never treated as zero.
- Net-worth trend ranges for one month, three months, one year, and all available history.

### Product Experience

- A top-level Activity route with cursor pagination and URL-backed filters.
- Type-specific Activity creation rather than a raw leg editor.
- Activity detail with linked reversal or correction records.
- Account timeline combining origin, Activities, relevant manual observations, and lifecycle changes.
- Overview net-worth trend with incomplete-point presentation and an accessible tabular alternative.
- English and Simplified Chinese copy for all workflows, classifications, errors, empty states, and warnings.

## Excluded

v0.1.3 does not implement:

- Cost-basis policy, tax lots, lot selection, realized or unrealized gain
- TWR, XIRR, benchmarks, investment-performance charts, or return attribution
- Automatic separation of market, FX, income, and fee attribution beyond recorded Activity classifications and snapshot provenance
- Fabricated historical trades, cash flows, or opening dates for v0.1.2 state
- Short positions, negative cash, margin, options, derivatives, or leveraged-position modeling
- Split, merger, spin-off, symbol-change, or other corporate-action processing
- Recurring or pending Activities, scheduled posting, reminders, or approval queues
- CSV import/export, statement import, backup/restore, sync, or multi-device collaboration
- Automatic bank or broker transaction synchronization
- Background snapshot generation while Nestworth is closed
- A live quote or FX vendor
- Permanent Activity deletion or in-place Activity editing

## Locked Product Decisions

| Decision | v0.1.3 contract |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger shape | One immutable Activity header with validated typed legs |
| Current-state projection | Activity and all Account/Holding/cash projection writes commit atomically |
| Existing Holding Quantity | Origin state only; never backfilled as a trade |
| Posted correction | New reversal, or atomic reversal plus replacement; no edit/delete |
| Absolute adjustments | Rust derives one additive increase/decrease leg from the current state; no absolute-set leg is persisted |
| Effective time | User supplies local date and time; Rust resolves it in the confirmed History Origin IANA timezone and stores UTC |
| Future posting | Rejected; pending/future Activities are deferred to v0.1.5 |
| Same-currency transfer | Source and destination currencies and amounts must match exactly |
| Cross-currency transfer | Requires exact source amount, destination amount, and explicit positive transaction FX rate |
| Position transfer | Same Instrument and exact Quantity on both legs; no gain or external flow |
| Buy/Sell settlement | Cash settlement currency equals the Instrument quote currency in v0.1.3 |
| Fees | Explicit, never hidden inside transfer or trade principal |
| Negative projected state | Rejected for Account balances, Holdings cash, liabilities, and Holding Quantity |
| Historical start | One persisted origin baseline; no pre-origin reconstructed trend |
| Daily snapshot | Closed local day, append-only revision, exact component and quote provenance |
| Missing historical quote | Exclude only affected component and mark snapshot incomplete |
| Snapshot rebuild | Explicit local application work; never a startup or provider prerequisite |
| Financial authority | Rust services only; frontend renders DTOs and chart geometry |
| Provider dependency | None; all Activity and history workflows remain manually operable offline |

## Activity Classification

| Activity kind | Primary effect | Wealth-flow classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening Adjustment | Establishes post-origin state for a new component | Remeasurement, not external flow |
| Balance or Position Adjustment | Reconciles current state to a user-entered fact | Remeasurement / unexplained change |
| Deposit | Increases one Household endpoint from outside | External inflow |
| Withdrawal | Decreases one Household endpoint to outside | External outflow |
| Transfer | Moves cash or a position between Household endpoints | Internal; zero external flow |
| Buy / Sell | Exchanges settlement cash and Quantity | Trade principal; zero external flow before fees |
| Income | Increases a cash or Balance endpoint | Income |
| Fee | Decreases a cash or Balance endpoint | Fee / expense |
| Debt Draw | Increases liability principal and, when paired, Household cash | Principal movement; paired draw is internal |
| Debt Payment | Decreases liability principal and Household cash | Principal movement; paired payment is internal |
| Debt Adjustment | Sets or changes liability without a paired cash movement | Remeasurement / unexplained change |
| Manual Valuation | Sets a Manual Value Account observation | Remeasurement, not external flow |

These classifications are durable facts for v0.1.4. v0.1.3 reports them but does not calculate investment return or attribution.

## Acceptance Scenarios

### Internal Cash Transfer

Starting state in CNY:

```text
Account A = 10,000
Account B =      0
Net worth = 10,000
```

Post a `3,000 CNY` internal transfer from A to B:

```text
Account A = 7,000
Account B = 3,000
Net worth = 10,000
External flow = 0
```

The Activity, both legs, both resulting Account observations, and snapshot invalidation must commit together or all write nothing.

### Trade and Fee

A Holdings Account contains `1,000 USD` cash and no QQQ. Post a Buy of `2 QQQ × 100 USD` with a `5 USD` fee:

```text
Settlement cash = 795 USD
QQQ Quantity = 2
Trade principal = 200 USD
Fee = 5 USD
External flow = 0
```

At an exact `100 USD` QQQ quote, the Account value is `995 USD`. The `5 USD` difference is the explicit Fee; the Buy itself does not create or destroy wealth.

### Cross-Currency Transfer

Transfer `100 USD` to `690 CNY` using the recorded transaction equation `1 USD = 6.9 CNY`. Both amounts and the explicit rate are preserved. The Activity is internal and has zero external flow at its transaction basis. Later snapshot movement caused by market FX is not reclassified as a deposit or withdrawal.

### Legacy Origin

A v0.1.2 Household opens v0.1.3 with an existing `3 QQQ` Holding and `5000 SGD` cash. Migration creates a History Origin and baseline components. It creates no Buy, Deposit, or Transfer. The first timeline row says history starts from existing state, and trend data before that origin is unavailable rather than invented.

### Incomplete Historical Snapshot

If a day has Holding Quantity but no Instrument Quote at or before the daily cutoff, that Holding is listed as missing, its value is excluded from the partial total, and the snapshot is `isComplete=false`. Adding a backdated manual quote invalidates the affected range and produces a new snapshot revision when rebuilt.

## Release Acceptance

v0.1.3 is complete only when:

- Migration `003` preserves v0.1.1/v0.1.2 identities, observations, current totals, and unsupported-future-database zero-write behavior.
- Every migrated Household has exactly one complete History Origin before Activity commands become available.
- An unresolved host timezone falls back to UTC but blocks Activity and snapshot writes until the user confirms UTC or another valid IANA timezone.
- Baseline capture creates zero Activities and cannot be mistaken for external flow or trade history.
- Every accepted Activity shape is validated in Rust and updates its projections atomically.
- Absolute adjustments and manual valuations persist a derived additive delta, so exact reversal remains valid after later Activities.
- A target equal to current state is rejected as no change; a zero-state Account or Holding may be created without fabricating an Opening Adjustment.
- Internal same-currency transfer and exact position transfer change participating Accounts while producing zero external flow.
- Cross-currency transfer retains both native amounts and the user-confirmed transaction rate.
- Buy and Sell update cash and Quantity atomically, and explicit fees remain distinguishable from principal.
- Debt principal, income, fees, adjustments, and valuations retain distinct classifications.
- A projected Account, cash, liability, or Holding value can never become negative.
- Posted Activities cannot be edited or deleted; reversal and correction preserve the complete chain.
- Activity filtering and Account timeline ordering are deterministic and cursor-paginated.
- Historical valuation never selects a quote after the snapshot cutoff.
- Daily snapshots retain component and quote provenance, preserve prior revisions, and expose incomplete diagnostics.
- Backdated facts mark the earliest affected local day dirty and rebuild only the required bounded range.
- Overview current value remains identical to the existing ValuationService result.
- Frontend code performs no balance, transfer, trade, net-worth, performance, FX reciprocal, or snapshot calculation.
- English and Simplified Chinese locale keys, generated IPC bindings, frozen command allowlist, CSP, logging, and error shielding are current.
- Domain, migration, origin, transaction, reversal, correction, historical valuation, snapshot, command, frontend, golden, and compatibility tests pass through `bun run check`.
- Isolated-data launch, keyboard navigation, VoiceOver smoke testing, arm64 packaging, and the chosen distribution policy pass the release gate.

## Compatibility Promises

v0.1.3 preserves every v0.1.1 and v0.1.2 promise and adds:

- Activity, Activity Leg, History Origin, and Snapshot identities remain stable; origin financial baseline rows never change.
- Posted Activities and snapshot revisions are immutable evidence.
- Current projection tables may be optimized later, but an Activity's recorded native facts and classifications may not be reinterpreted.
- A baseline item never becomes a fabricated Activity in a later migration.
- An Adjustment remains distinguishable from an external flow and from a trade.
- Transfer links survive Account or Instrument archive and remain resolvable.
- Trade execution Quantity, Unit Price, gross settlement amount, settlement currency, and explicit fees remain available for v0.1.4 cost-basis work.
- v0.1.4 may add lot policy and attribution but must not infer missing trades or silently assign cost basis to origin or adjustment quantities.
- Historical snapshot provenance remains distinguishable from Activity facts; a valuation revision does not rewrite the Activity ledger.
